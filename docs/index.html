Before you begin

This is a fictional sports league simulation using study credits; no real money is involved. Each week features one game to bet on while a full 12-team schedule is played. Bets are capped at 500 credits and cannot exceed available credits. When available credits fall below one, you may take another 1,000-credit loan; loans remain as debt during the simulation and winnings do not repay them automatically, so your net position can become negative. Some details about how results are determined will be explained after the simulation.

The site does not ask for your name or contact details. Do not enter any personal information.

Responses are sent to the study server when you finish. For questions about the research, contact the researcher who shared this link.

Study explanation

The study examines whether a run of wins or losses is followed by a change in bet size. The featured game outcomes in weeks 5–8 were assigned to create the sequence. Other game outcomes were simulated using the displayed odds, which also influence score margins.
